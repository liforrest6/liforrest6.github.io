How has maize diversity been shaped by human culture in Mesoamerica?  We leverage human ethnolinguistic and genetic data to answer questions about patterns of maize migration and selection.

In collaboration with Samantha Snodgrass, I contributed analyses using linguistic relationship distances from the Glottolog ASJP database to correlate an ethnolinguistic proxy to maize genetic distance in Mesoamerica.  Further, I identify patterns of gene flow/migration along the geographic range.  What does this mean in relation to how local ethnolinguistic groups utilize and breed maize?
